NOVAPAY DIGITAL BANK
FIXED DEPOSIT (FD) POLICY
Document ID: POL-FD-007 | Version 2.0 | Effective: January 1, 2026

1. FIXED DEPOSIT PRODUCTS
1.1 NovaPay Regular FD: Standard fixed deposit with guaranteed returns
1.2 NovaPay Tax Saver FD: 5-year lock-in FD eligible for tax deduction under Section 80C of Income Tax Act
1.3 NovaPay Flexi FD: FD linked to savings account; automatic sweep-in/sweep-out for liquidity
1.4 NovaPay Senior Citizen FD: Additional 0.50% interest rate for customers aged 60 and above

2. ELIGIBILITY
2.1 All NovaPay savings account holders with Full KYC status are eligible to open Fixed Deposits.
2.2 Minimum KYC customers are not eligible for Fixed Deposits.
2.3 Joint FDs are permitted; interest and maturity proceeds follow the savings account of the primary holder.

3. DEPOSIT PARAMETERS
3.1 Minimum deposit amount: INR 10,000
3.2 Maximum deposit amount: No upper limit (subject to DICGC insurance cap of INR 5,00,000 per depositor)
3.3 Tenure: Minimum 7 days; Maximum 10 years
3.4 Interest payment options: Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, or Cumulative (at maturity)

4. INTEREST RATES (Effective January 1, 2026)
4.1 7 days to 29 days: 4.00% per annum
4.2 30 days to 90 days: 5.00% per annum
4.3 91 days to 180 days: 5.75% per annum
4.4 181 days to 364 days: 6.25% per annum
4.5 1 year to 2 years: 7.00% per annum
4.6 2 years to 5 years: 7.25% per annum
4.7 5 years to 10 years: 7.00% per annum
4.8 Senior Citizens: Additional 0.50% on all tenures above 180 days
4.9 Tax Saver FD (5 years): 7.50% per annum (fixed, not subject to rate revisions during tenure)

5. PREMATURE WITHDRAWAL
5.1 Regular FD: Premature withdrawal is permitted at any time. Penalty: 1% reduction on the applicable rate for the period the FD was held.
5.2 Tax Saver FD: Premature withdrawal is NOT permitted under any circumstances (5-year mandatory lock-in as per Income Tax Act).
5.3 Flexi FD: Withdrawal up to the sweep-in amount is penalty-free; withdrawal beyond sweep-in amount attracts standard premature withdrawal penalty.
5.4 Senior Citizen FD: 0.50% penalty waiver on premature withdrawal in case of medical emergency (supporting documents required).
5.5 FDs opened for less than 7 days: No interest paid on premature withdrawal.

6. AUTO-RENEWAL
6.1 By default, all Regular and Senior Citizen FDs auto-renew at maturity for the same tenure at the prevailing interest rate on the date of renewal.
6.2 Customers can opt out of auto-renewal via the NovaPay app or by providing written instruction at least 3 working days before maturity.
6.3 If auto-renewal is opted out, maturity proceeds are credited to the linked savings account on the maturity date.

7. TDS (TAX DEDUCTED AT SOURCE)
7.1 TDS is deducted at 10% if total FD interest in a financial year exceeds INR 40,000 (INR 50,000 for Senior Citizens).
7.2 Customers with income below taxable limit must submit Form 15G (below 60 years) or Form 15H (60 years and above) to avoid TDS deduction.
7.3 Form 15G/15H must be submitted at the beginning of each financial year.
7.4 TDS certificates (Form 16A) are available for download from the NovaPay app quarterly.

8. NOMINATION
8.1 Customers are strongly advised to register a nominee for each Fixed Deposit.
8.2 Nomination can be added, changed, or removed via the NovaPay app or branch.
8.3 In the event of the depositor's death, the nominee may claim the FD proceeds by submitting: death certificate, nominee's identity proof, and a claim form at any NovaPay branch.

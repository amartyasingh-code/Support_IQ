NOVAPAY DIGITAL BANK
CREDIT CARD REFUND AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION POLICY
Document ID: POL-CC-002 | Version 1.8 | Effective: January 1, 2026

1. SCOPE
This policy applies to all NovaPay credit card holders (NovaPay Classic Card, NovaPay Rewards Card, NovaPay Platinum Card) for disputed transactions, refund claims, and chargeback requests.

2. TYPES OF DISPUTES COVERED
2.1 Unauthorized transactions — transactions not initiated by the cardholder
2.2 Merchant disputes — goods/services not received, not as described, or defective
2.3 Duplicate charges — same transaction charged more than once
2.4 Incorrect amount — amount charged differs from the agreed purchase amount
2.5 ATM disputes — cash not dispensed but account debited

3. REPORTING TIMELINE
3.1 Customers must report disputed transactions within 30 days of the transaction date for domestic transactions.
3.2 For international transactions, the reporting window is 45 days from the transaction date.
3.3 Disputes raised beyond these timelines will be reviewed at NovaPay's discretion and may not be eligible for chargeback.
3.4 Unauthorized transaction disputes must be reported immediately (within 3 days) to qualify for zero liability protection.

4. ZERO LIABILITY PROTECTION
4.1 Customers are not liable for unauthorized transactions IF:
    - The card was reported lost/stolen before the transaction occurred, OR
    - The transaction occurred due to NovaPay system error, OR
    - The dispute is reported within 3 days of the unauthorized transaction
4.2 Customer liability for unauthorized transactions reported between 4–7 days: Maximum INR 10,000.
4.3 Customer liability for unauthorized transactions reported after 7 days: Up to full transaction amount.

5. DISPUTE RESOLUTION PROCESS
5.1 Step 1: Customer raises dispute via NovaPay app, customer support helpline (1800-XXX-XXXX), or email (disputes@novapay.in).
5.2 Step 2: NovaPay issues a provisional credit within 10 working days for disputes under INR 50,000.
5.3 Step 3: NovaPay investigates the dispute with the merchant/payment network (Visa/Mastercard). Investigation period: up to 45 working days.
5.4 Step 4: Final resolution communicated to customer. If dispute is upheld, provisional credit becomes permanent. If rejected, provisional credit is reversed with explanation.

6. REFUND PROCESSING
6.1 Merchant-initiated refunds (returns/cancellations) are credited to the credit card account within 5–10 working days of merchant processing.
6.2 Refunds do not earn reward points.
6.3 If the original transaction earned reward points, those points will be reversed upon refund.
6.4 Refunds on EMI transactions: The outstanding EMI amount will be adjusted; processing fee is non-refundable.

7. CHARGEBACK RIGHTS
7.1 NovaPay will initiate a chargeback with Visa/Mastercard on the customer's behalf if the merchant fails to resolve the dispute within 15 days.
7.2 Chargeback success is subject to Visa/Mastercard network rules and is not guaranteed.
7.3 If chargeback is rejected by the network, NovaPay will communicate the reason and any further escalation options.

8. ESCALATION
8.1 If unsatisfied with the resolution, customers may escalate to the NovaPay Grievance Officer within 30 days of the final resolution communication.
8.2 Further escalation: RBI Banking Ombudsman (bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in).

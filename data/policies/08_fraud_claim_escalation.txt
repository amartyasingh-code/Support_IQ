NOVAPAY DIGITAL BANK
FRAUD CLAIM AND ESCALATION POLICY
Document ID: POL-FRD-008 | Version 2.2 | Effective: January 1, 2026

1. DEFINITION OF FRAUD
For the purposes of this policy, fraud includes:
1.1 Unauthorized access to customer accounts via phishing, SIM swapping, or social engineering
1.2 Unauthorized card transactions (physical card theft, card skimming, CNP — Card Not Present fraud)
1.3 Unauthorized UPI/NEFT/IMPS/RTGS transactions initiated without customer consent
1.4 Identity theft — fraudulent account opening using customer's identity
1.5 Internal fraud — unauthorized transactions by NovaPay employees (handled under separate internal policy)

2. IMMEDIATE STEPS FOR FRAUD VICTIMS
2.1 Block all cards immediately via NovaPay app (Cards → Block Card) or call 1800-XXX-XXXX.
2.2 Change UPI PIN and internet banking password immediately.
2.3 Report the fraud to NovaPay fraud team within 3 days for zero liability protection eligibility.
2.4 File a complaint with Cybercrime portal (cybercrime.gov.in) or local police station.
2.5 Preserve all evidence: SMS alerts, email communications, call recordings if any.

3. REPORTING FRAUD TO NOVAPAY
3.1 Channels:
    - Emergency Fraud Hotline: 1800-XXX-YYYY (24x7, dedicated fraud response team)
    - Email: fraud@novapay.in (response within 2 hours during business hours)
    - NovaPay app: Help → Report Fraud
    - Branch visit with written complaint

3.2 Information required:
    - Account number / card number
    - Fraudulent transaction details (date, amount, transaction ID)
    - How the fraud occurred (if known)
    - Police complaint number (if filed)

4. FRAUD INVESTIGATION PROCESS
4.1 NovaPay acknowledges fraud report within 2 hours (business hours) or by next working day.
4.2 Provisional credit (for eligible cases) issued within 10 working days.
4.3 Full investigation completed within 45 working days as per RBI mandate.
4.4 Customer kept informed of investigation progress at minimum every 15 working days.

5. LIABILITY FRAMEWORK (AS PER RBI CIRCULAR RBI/2017-18/15)
5.1 Zero customer liability when:
    - Fraud due to NovaPay system or employee negligence (regardless of reporting time)
    - Fraud reported within 3 working days of the fraudulent transaction
    - Third-party breach where customer has not contributed to the fraud

5.2 Limited customer liability (reported 4–7 working days after fraud):
    - Transactions up to INR 10,000: Zero liability
    - Transactions INR 10,001 to INR 1,00,000: Maximum liability INR 10,000
    - Transactions above INR 1,00,000: Maximum liability INR 25,000

5.3 Full customer liability when:
    - Customer shared credentials (PIN, OTP, password) with anyone
    - Fraud reported after 7 working days
    - Customer negligence contributed to the fraud

6. ESCALATION FRAMEWORK
6.1 Level 1: NovaPay Fraud Response Team (first point of contact, resolution target: 45 working days)
6.2 Level 2: NovaPay Grievance Officer (if dissatisfied with Level 1 resolution; within 30 days of final response)
    - Email: grievance@novapay.in
    - Written complaint to: Grievance Officer, NovaPay Digital Bank, [Address]
    - Resolution target: 30 days
6.3 Level 3: RBI Banking Ombudsman (if unresolved after Level 2 or no response within 30 days)
    - Portal: bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in
    - No fee for filing complaint with Ombudsman
6.4 Level 4: Consumer Court or Civil Court (legal remedy)

7. FRAUD PREVENTION TIPS
7.1 NovaPay will NEVER ask for OTP, PIN, CVV, or full card number via phone or email.
7.2 Always verify UPI payment requests before approving — collecting money never requires UPI PIN entry.
7.3 Enable transaction alerts for all transactions above INR 1 via NovaPay app settings.
7.4 Regularly review account statements and report discrepancies immediately.
7.5 Use strong, unique passwords for NovaPay internet banking and change them every 90 days.

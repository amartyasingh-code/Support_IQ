NOVAPAY DIGITAL BANK
SAVINGS ACCOUNT TERMS AND CONDITIONS
Document ID: POL-SAV-001 | Version 2.4 | Effective: January 1, 2026

1. ACCOUNT ELIGIBILITY
1.1 NovaPay Savings Accounts are available to Indian residents aged 18 years and above.
1.2 Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) are not eligible for standard savings accounts and must apply for NRE/NRO accounts separately.
1.3 Each customer may hold a maximum of 3 individual savings accounts with NovaPay Digital Bank.
1.4 Joint accounts are permitted with a maximum of 3 account holders.

2. MINIMUM BALANCE REQUIREMENTS
2.1 NovaPay Zero Balance Account: No minimum balance required. Available to all verified KYC customers.
2.2 NovaPay Classic Savings Account: Minimum Average Monthly Balance (AMB) of INR 5,000.
2.3 NovaPay Premium Savings Account: Minimum Average Monthly Balance (AMB) of INR 25,000.
2.4 Non-maintenance of minimum balance will attract a penalty charge as follows:
    - Classic Account: INR 150 per month for shortfall up to 50%; INR 300 per month for shortfall above 50%
    - Premium Account: INR 500 per month for shortfall up to 50%; INR 750 per month for shortfall above 50%

3. INTEREST RATES
3.1 Interest on savings account balances is calculated on a daily basis and credited quarterly.
3.2 Current interest rates (subject to change with 30 days notice):
    - Balance up to INR 1,00,000: 3.50% per annum
    - Balance INR 1,00,001 to INR 10,00,000: 4.00% per annum
    - Balance above INR 10,00,000: 4.25% per annum
3.3 Interest rates are reviewed by the Asset Liability Management Committee (ALCO) quarterly.

4. TRANSACTION LIMITS
4.1 ATM cash withdrawals: Maximum INR 50,000 per day (Classic); INR 1,00,000 per day (Premium).
4.2 UPI transactions: Maximum INR 1,00,000 per transaction; INR 2,00,000 per day.
4.3 NEFT/RTGS: No upper limit; subject to RBI guidelines.
4.4 IMPS: Maximum INR 5,00,000 per transaction.
4.5 International transactions: Disabled by default; must be enabled via app or branch request.

5. ACCOUNT CLOSURE
5.1 Customers may request account closure at any time via the NovaPay app, customer support, or branch visit.
5.2 Accounts closed within 12 months of opening will attract a closure fee of INR 500.
5.3 Zero Balance Accounts: No closure fee at any time.
5.4 All pending transactions must be settled before closure. Outstanding dues will be recovered from the closing balance.
5.5 Remaining balance will be transferred to the customer's linked account within 5 working days of closure.

6. DORMANCY POLICY
6.1 An account with no customer-initiated transactions for 12 consecutive months is classified as Inactive.
6.2 An account with no customer-initiated transactions for 24 consecutive months is classified as Dormant.
6.3 Dormant accounts are frozen for debit transactions only; interest continues to accrue.
6.4 To reactivate a dormant account, customers must visit a NovaPay branch with valid KYC documents.

7. DEPOSIT INSURANCE
7.1 All NovaPay savings account deposits are insured by the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) up to INR 5,00,000 per depositor per bank.

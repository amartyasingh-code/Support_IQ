NOVAPAY DIGITAL BANK
UPI TRANSACTION DISPUTE RESOLUTION POLICY
Document ID: POL-UPI-003 | Version 2.1 | Effective: January 1, 2026

1. OVERVIEW
NovaPay Digital Bank is a member of the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) UPI ecosystem. This policy governs dispute resolution for UPI transactions initiated through the NovaPay app or any UPI-enabled application linked to a NovaPay account.

2. COMMON UPI DISPUTE CATEGORIES
2.1 Transaction pending but amount debited — transaction shows pending on UPI but account has been debited
2.2 Amount debited but not credited to beneficiary — money left sender's account but did not reach recipient
2.3 Incorrect beneficiary credited — wrong UPI ID or mobile number used
2.4 Duplicate transaction — same payment processed twice
2.5 Fraudulent/unauthorized transaction — transaction initiated without customer's knowledge or consent
2.6 Transaction declined but amount debited — transaction failed but amount was deducted

3. TRANSACTION TIMELINES AND AUTO-REVERSAL
3.1 Failed UPI transactions where the amount has been debited are automatically reversed within:
    - Same bank transactions (NovaPay to NovaPay): Within 1 working day
    - Inter-bank transactions: Within 3 working days
    - Technical failure cases: Within 5 working days
3.2 If auto-reversal does not occur within the above timelines, customers must raise a formal dispute.

4. RAISING A UPI DISPUTE
4.1 Disputes can be raised through:
    - NovaPay app: Go to Transaction History → Select Transaction → Raise Dispute
    - Customer Support: 1800-XXX-XXXX (24x7 for UPI disputes)
    - Email: upi-disputes@novapay.in
4.2 Required information for dispute:
    - Transaction ID (UPI Reference Number / UTR)
    - Transaction date and amount
    - Beneficiary UPI ID or mobile number
    - Nature of dispute

5. RESOLUTION TIMELINES (AS PER NPCI MANDATE)
5.1 NovaPay acknowledges the dispute within 24 hours of receipt.
5.2 Resolution timelines by category:
    - Pending transactions / amount not credited: 3 working days
    - Duplicate transactions: 5 working days
    - Fraudulent transactions: 7 working days (investigation required)
    - Incorrect beneficiary: Up to 30 working days (requires beneficiary bank cooperation)
5.3 If NovaPay is unable to resolve within the mandated timeline, a provisional credit may be issued pending investigation.

6. INCORRECT BENEFICIARY TRANSACTIONS
6.1 If money is sent to an incorrect UPI ID, NovaPay will initiate a retrieval request with the beneficiary's bank.
6.2 Recovery is subject to the beneficiary's consent and cooperation. NovaPay cannot force recovery without a court order.
6.3 If the beneficiary refuses to return the funds, customers are advised to file a complaint with the cybercrime portal (cybercrime.gov.in) or local police.
6.4 NovaPay will provide all transaction details required for legal proceedings.

7. FRAUD PREVENTION AND CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITIES
7.1 Customers must never share UPI PIN, OTP, or linked account credentials with anyone, including NovaPay representatives.
7.2 NovaPay representatives will never ask for UPI PIN or OTP.
7.3 Customers who share credentials are liable for resulting losses; zero liability protection does not apply.
7.4 Customers are advised to enable transaction limits and SMS alerts for all UPI transactions.

8. COMPENSATION FOR DELAYED RESOLUTION
8.1 As per RBI guidelines, if NovaPay fails to resolve a valid UPI dispute within the mandated timeline, customers are entitled to compensation of INR 100 per day of delay, up to a maximum of INR 10,000.
8.2 Compensation is credited automatically to the customer's linked savings account.
